隱私權政策

Arrow Planet・7 Studio・最後更新 2026-11-15

簡單說

Arrow Planet 不需要帳號，我們自己不收集、也不保存任何能認出你的資料。遊戲是免費的，第 11 關起會在關卡開始前播放廣告；廣告由第三方廣告服務提供，這些服務會為了投放廣告而處理下面列出的裝置資料。

廣告服務處理的資料

遊戲使用 Unity LevelPlay（含 ironSource Ads 與 Unity Ads，皆屬 Unity Technologies）播放廣告。播放廣告時，這些服務可能會處理：

  • 裝置識別碼：供應商識別碼（IDFV）等。遊戲不會要求「允許追蹤」的權限，所以廣告服務拿不到你的廣告識別碼（IDFA），也不會把你在這個遊戲裡的資料和其他公司的 App 或網站的資料連起來追蹤你。
  • 裝置與網路資訊：裝置型號、作業系統版本、語言、地區、IP 位址（可推得大概的國家或城市，不是精確位置）、網路類型。
  • 廣告互動：播放了哪則廣告、是否看完或點擊，以及用於防止詐欺與統計成效的診斷資訊。

這些資料由廣告服務依其隱私權政策處理與保存：Unity Privacy Policy。在歐盟、歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士，遊戲會告訴廣告服務「未取得個人化廣告的同意」，因此只會顯示非個人化廣告。

訂閱

「移除廣告」是透過 Apple 的 App 內購買進行的訂閱。付款由 Apple 處理，我們不會收到你的姓名、付款方式或 Apple 帳號資料，只會從你的裝置得知訂閱是否有效。訂閱期間遊戲不會播放廣告。

我們不收集什麼

姓名、Email、電話、通訊錄、照片、精確位置、健康資料，或你輸入的任何內容。遊戲沒有帳號系統、沒有聊天、沒有排行榜，也沒有我們自己的分析或當機回報服務。

存在你裝置上的資料

遊戲進度、星星、設定與語言只存在你的裝置上。刪除 App 就會一併刪除。若你使用 iCloud 裝置備份，Apple 可能會依你的設定備份這些資料。

兒童

本遊戲不是專為 13 歲以下兒童設計的，我們也不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資料。

你的選擇

訂閱「移除廣告」後不再播放廣告。你也可以在 iOS 的「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」與「Apple 廣告」中調整廣告相關設定。想查詢或刪除廣告服務持有的資料，請依上方 Unity 隱私權政策中的方式提出。

政策變更與聯絡方式

若未來版本改變上述做法，我們會先更新本頁。有任何問題，請到 GitHub Issues 留言。

Privacy Policy

Arrow Planet · 7 Studio · last updated 2026-11-15

In short

Arrow Planet needs no account, and we ourselves collect and keep nothing that identifies you. The game is free; from level 11 an ad plays before each level. The ads come from third-party advertising services, which process the device data listed below in order to show them.

Data processed by the advertising services

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (with ironSource Ads and Unity Ads, all part of Unity Technologies). When an ad is shown, these services may process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the identifier for vendors (IDFV). The game does not ask for permission to track, so the advertising services do not receive your advertising identifier (IDFA) and do not link what you do in this game with data from other companies' apps or websites to track you.
  • Device and network information: device model, OS version, language, region, IP address (from which a country or city can be inferred, not a precise location) and connection type.
  • Ad interaction: which ad was shown, whether it was watched or tapped, and diagnostics used to prevent fraud and measure performance.

The advertising services process and keep this data under their own policy: Unity Privacy Policy. In the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game tells the advertising services that consent for personalised ads has not been given, so only non-personalised ads are shown.

Subscription

“Remove Ads” is a subscription bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your name, payment method or Apple Account details, only whether the subscription is active on your device. No ads are shown while it is active.

What we do not collect

Names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, precise location, health data, or anything you type. The game has no accounts, no chat, no leaderboards, and no analytics or crash reporting service of our own.

Data kept on your device

Your progress, stars, settings and language are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. If you use iCloud device backup, Apple may back them up according to your settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Subscribing to “Remove Ads” stops the ads. You can also change advertising settings in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Apple Advertising. To ask about or delete data held by the advertising services, use the routes given in the Unity Privacy Policy above.

Changes and contact

If a future version changes any of this, we will update this page first. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
