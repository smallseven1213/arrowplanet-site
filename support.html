02 — SUPPORT

客服與常見問題

遇到問題或有建議，請到 GitHub Issues 開一則回報，並附上裝置型號、iOS 版本與發生的情況（可附截圖）。

怎麼玩？

點一支箭頭，它會往前滑到邊緣、飛進宇宙。前方有別的箭頭擋著就走不了，要先把擋路的送走。每關可以點錯三次。用一根手指拖曳可以轉動星球。

箭頭上的記號是什麼意思？

多一個尖頭：要先落到另一顆星球才能離開。箭頭後面的橫槓：會多翻過幾個邊才飛走。虛線箭頭：被鎖住了，要先送走同色的鑰匙箭頭。按住箭頭不放，可以看到它會怎麼走。

怎麼移除廣告？

在選關畫面右上角點「移除廣告」，訂閱後就不再播放廣告。訂閱按月自動續訂，可隨時在 iPhone 的「設定 → 你的名字 → 訂閱項目」取消。

換手機或重裝後訂閱不見了？

在「移除廣告」頁面點「恢復購買」，用原本購買的 Apple 帳號即可恢復。

進度存在哪裡？

自動存在你的裝置上。刪除 App 會刪除進度。

Support & FAQ

Problems or ideas? Open an issue on GitHub with your device model, iOS version and what happened (screenshots help).

How do I play?

Tap an arrow and it slides forward to the edge and flies off into space. It cannot leave while another arrow is in its way, so send that one first. Three mistakes are allowed per level. Drag with one finger to turn the planet.

What do the marks on arrows mean?

Extra heads: the arrow lands on another planet before it can leave. Bars behind the head: it goes round that many edges before flying off. Dashed arrows are locked until the key arrow of the same colour has gone. Hold an arrow to see the path it will take.

How do I remove the ads?

Tap “Remove Ads” at the top right of the level map and subscribe. The subscription renews monthly and can be cancelled at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

My subscription is gone after reinstalling

Open “Remove Ads” and tap “Restore Purchase” while signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

Where is my progress?

On your device, saved automatically. Deleting the app deletes it.
