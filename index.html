Arrow Planet

玻璃星球上佈滿發光的箭頭。點一下，箭頭就往前滑、飛進宇宙；前方有別的箭頭擋著就走不了。把每一支都送走，星球就清空了。
Glass planets covered in glowing arrows. Tap one and it slides off into space, unless another arrow is in its way. Send every arrow away to clear the planet. A puzzle game for iPhone by 7 Studio.
